Clairic and Clairic Flash use the existing qwen35 hybrid-cache model. Their
publisher's structured-output helpers require Transformers and XGrammar.
Shot Reader is a Qwen3-VL checkpoint with a merged adapter. Its Qwen3-VL
backbone has upstream support in b12409. The publisher's shot-reading
pipeline samples 16 frames and has additional preprocessing/runtime costs.
The calculator sizes the recorded weight and projector files with the
selected token context. It does not predict that pipeline's peak memory.

At 4K, f16 KV, batch 1 and text only, Q4_K_M totals are 17.80, 6.08 and
5.87 GiB respectively. These are estimates. No measurements, per-quant
speed, perplexity loss or quality figures were added.
